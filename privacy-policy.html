1. Who We Are

DoneStead Property Solutions Ltd ("DoneStead", "we", "us", "our") is a property maintenance coordination company registered in England and Wales (company number 17302302). Our registered office is at 18, 149 Blackfriars Road, London SE1 8EF.

We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) as a data controller under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. ICO registration number: [Complete before launch — register at ico.org.uk].

For all data protection matters: info@donestead.co.uk  |  07867 776 421

2. What Personal Data We Collect and Why

2.1 Providing our services

What: Name, job title, company name, email, phone, property addresses, maintenance details, photographs, job history, communication records.
Why: To coordinate maintenance services, process instructions, produce photo reports and compliance documents, and communicate about active jobs.
Lawful basis: Contract — necessary to perform the services you have requested.

2.2 Quotations and enquiries

What: Name, company name, contact details, property information, photographs submitted via Photo Quote.
Why: To prepare quotations and respond to enquiries.
Lawful basis: Legitimate interests — following up on business enquiries and providing accurate quotations.

2.3 Compliance and legal obligations

What: Company details, payment records, contractor registration details (for CIS).
Why: HMRC Construction Industry Scheme compliance and statutory record-keeping.
Lawful basis: Legal obligation.

2.4 Client portal and account management

What: Login credentials (encrypted), account preferences, job history, document access logs.
Why: To operate the Client Portal.
Lawful basis: Contract — providing the portal is part of our service agreement.

2.5 Awaab Check tool

What: Email, property address, photographs of damp or mould issues.
Why: To provide a compliance-ready damp and mould risk assessment.

⚠ [Action required] Confirm with a data protection specialist whether photographs of residential interiors constitute special category data where health conditions of occupants may be visible. If so, document explicit consent as an additional lawful basis.

Automated processing: Photographs are initially pre-screened by an automated system, then reviewed and approved by a member of the DoneStead coordination team. The automated system does not make final decisions — every assessment is approved by a human reviewer before it is communicated to you. You have the right to request human review of any assessment under UK GDPR Article 22.

2.6 Marketing communications

What: Name, company name, email address.
Why: To send service updates and relevant offers.
Lawful basis: Consent (new contacts); legitimate interests (existing clients). You may opt out at any time by emailing info@donestead.co.uk or clicking unsubscribe in any email.

2.7 Website analytics

What: IP address, browser type, pages visited (collected via analytics cookies).
Why: To understand how visitors use our website and improve it.
Lawful basis: Consent — analytics cookies are only set with your permission via our cookie banner.

3. Contractor Data

We hold personal data about our Partner Contractors, including: name, business name, contact details, trade registration numbers (Gas Safe, NAPIT, NICEIC), bank account details for CIS payments, and records of work carried out. We process contractor data on the basis of contract and legal obligation (CIS). Contractor data is retained for 7 years for tax compliance purposes.

4. Who We Share Your Data With

Partner Contractors: We share relevant property details and instructions with the contractors attending your property — only the minimum necessary to carry out the work.

IT and platform providers: Third-party providers for hosting, email, job management and analytics process data on our behalf as data processors, under written Data Processing Agreements.

⚠ [Action required] List each provider by name and country: hosting — 17302302, email — 17302302, FSM platform — 17302302, analytics — 17302302. Confirm transfer safeguards for any non-UK processors.

HMRC: Contractor payment information under the Construction Industry Scheme.

Professional advisers: Solicitors, accountants and insurers under confidentiality obligations.

Legal proceedings: Where required by law, court order, or regulatory authority.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share data with advertisers or use it for advertising profiling.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Client contact and account records: 7 years from last transaction (HMRC / legal compliance)
  • Job records and photo reports: 7 years from job completion
  • Awaab and compliance assessments: 7 years from assessment date
  • Marketing contact records: 3 years from last engagement, or until consent withdrawn
  • Website enquiries (no contract follows): 2 years
  • Contractor records: 7 years from last payment (HMRC CIS)
  • Job photography: 7 years from job completion

After the applicable period, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

7. Cookies

Our website uses cookies. Essential cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be disabled. Analytics cookies help us understand how our website is used and are only set with your consent. Marketing cookies are only set with your consent. You can manage cookie preferences at any time using our cookie settings tool. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

8. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

  • Access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Restriction: Ask us to limit processing in certain circumstances
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing
  • Automated decisions: Request human review of any automated assessment
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw consent at any time where processing is consent-based

To exercise any right, contact info@donestead.co.uk. We will respond within one calendar month. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk | 0303 123 1113.

9. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted storage, access controls, and secure document transmission. In the event of a data breach posing a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the ICO within 72 hours and affected individuals without undue delay.

10. Children's Data

Our services are intended for business clients and professional landlords. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If you believe we have received such data, please contact us immediately.

11. Legitimate Interests

Where we rely on legitimate interests as our lawful basis, we have conducted a Legitimate Interests Assessment to confirm our interests are not overridden by your rights. A copy is available on request.

12. Changes to This Policy

We review this Privacy Policy at least annually. The current version is always available at www.donestead.co.uk/privacy-policy/. We will notify active clients of material changes by email.
