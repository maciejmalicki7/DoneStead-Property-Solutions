Cookie Policy

How DoneStead uses cookies on our website and how you can manage your preferences.

Effective date: [INSERT DATE] Last reviewed: July 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device, remember your preferences, and gather usage information.

2. Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are strictly necessary for the website to work. They cannot be disabled.

  • session_id — maintains your session when using the Client Portal. Duration: session.
  • csrf_token — security cookie, prevents cross-site request forgery. Duration: session.
  • cookie_consent — stores your cookie preferences. Duration: 12 months.

2.2 Analytics Cookies (Consent Required)

These help us understand how visitors use our website. They collect information in anonymised, aggregated form.

  • _ga — Google Analytics, distinguishes users. Duration: 2 years.
  • _ga_[ID] — Google Analytics, session state. Duration: 2 years.
[Action required] Add all analytics tools used. Ensure Google Analytics is configured with IP anonymisation enabled.

2.3 Marketing Cookies (Consent Required)

We do not currently use marketing cookies. If we introduce them, this policy will be updated and your consent will be requested again.

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Our website: Update your preferences at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the website footer.

Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Note that disabling essential cookies will affect website functionality. For guidance: Chrome — Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies; Firefox — Options > Privacy & Security; Safari — Preferences > Privacy; Edge — Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions.

Google Analytics opt-out: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some pages may contain content from third parties that set their own cookies. We do not control these. Please refer to the relevant third party's privacy and cookie policies.

5. Changes to This Policy

We update this Cookie Policy when we change the cookies we use. The current version is always available at www.donestead.co.uk/cookie-policy/.
