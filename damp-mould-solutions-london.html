Awaab's Law — What It Means for You

The Renters' Rights Act 2025 and Awaab's Law impose strict response timelines for damp and mould in social and private rented properties. Failure to comply carries financial and legal consequences.

  • 14-day maximum response from hazard report
  • 10-day Awaab protocol for serious damp cases
  • Written remediation plan required within deadlines
  • Photographic evidence and time-stamped audit trail
  • Compliance certificate on completion
  • Applies to social AND private rented sector
