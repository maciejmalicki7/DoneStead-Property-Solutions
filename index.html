Certification & Partner Disclosures
Gas Safety Services (CP12)
Gas Safety Certificate inspections and all gas appliance work at DoneStead-managed properties are carried out exclusively by Gas Safe registered engineers. DoneStead Property Solutions Ltd acts as a management coordinator. The CP12 record is issued by the registered engineer and is legally valid under the Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998. Landlords receive their certificate within 28 days of inspection.
Electrical Safety Services (EICR)
Electrical Installation Condition Reports and all notifiable electrical work are carried out exclusively by NAPIT or NICEIC registered electricians, satisfying the Electrical Safety Standards in the Private Rented Sector (England) Regulations 2020. DoneStead coordinates scheduling, access and documentation. Certificates are issued directly by the registered electrician.
