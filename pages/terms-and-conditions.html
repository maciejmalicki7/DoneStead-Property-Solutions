1. Definitions

"DoneStead" means DoneStead Property Solutions Ltd, registered in England and Wales (company number 17302302), registered office at 18, 149 Blackfriars Road, London SE1 8EF.

"Client" means the business, organisation or professional landlord entering into an agreement with DoneStead for the provision of Services.

"Services" means property maintenance coordination, managed end-of-tenancy preparation, damp surveys and remediation, compliance certificate coordination, refurbishment coordination, photo assessment, and related services provided by DoneStead as described on our website or in any written Quotation.

"Instruction" means a confirmed written order for Services submitted by the Client by email, the Client Portal, or another written method accepted by DoneStead.

"Partner Contractor" means a vetted, independent third-party trade contractor engaged by DoneStead to carry out physical work at a Client's property.

"Quotation" means a written price provided by DoneStead for a specific, agreed scope of Services.

"Photo Assessment" means the Photo Quote and Awaab Check services through which the Client submits photographs and receives a price indication or compliance assessment.

"Works" means any physical work carried out at a property under an Instruction.

"Deliverables" means documents, reports, photographs, assessments and compliance records produced by DoneStead in connection with the Services.

2. Nature of DoneStead's Services

✦ Revised v3.0: Clause 2 has been rewritten to clearly confirm DoneStead's role as principal contractor, set out its coordination obligations explicitly, and expand the limitations and disclaimers applicable to Photo Assessments and Awaab Check to address professional liability risk.

2.1 Principal contractor model

DoneStead provides property maintenance coordination services and contracts with Clients as principal. DoneStead engages Partner Contractors in its own name and on its own account. The Client's contractual relationship is with DoneStead; the Client has no direct contractual relationship with any Partner Contractor unless DoneStead otherwise agrees in writing.

This means:

  • DoneStead is responsible to the Client for the delivery of the Services as described in these Terms and in the agreed scope;
  • DoneStead is not responsible for defects or damage arising from the wilful misconduct or gross negligence of a Partner Contractor that is not within DoneStead's reasonable ability to prevent, subject to clause 12;
  • DoneStead selects Partner Contractors using reasonable care and maintains a vetting process for its contractor panel;
  • DoneStead's invoices to Clients reflect the full coordinated service price, inclusive of DoneStead's management and coordination fee.
Why this matters to the Client: The Client deals with one company (DoneStead), receives one invoice, and has one point of contact for quality and complaints — regardless of which Partner Contractor carries out the physical work.

2.2 DoneStead's coordination obligations

In delivering the Services, DoneStead will:

  1. scope and agree Works with the Client before instructing any Partner Contractor;
  2. select Partner Contractors from its vetted panel, verifying their relevant trade registrations (Gas Safe, NAPIT/NICEIC, or other applicable body) before each regulated job;
  3. brief the Partner Contractor on the agreed scope and access arrangements;
  4. obtain before-and-after photographic evidence on every job and review it before sharing with the Client;
  5. provide a written completion report and raise any issues identified during the Works for the Client's attention;
  6. manage defects notified within the defect liability period under clause 7.

2.3 Partner Contractors — independent status

Partner Contractors are independent businesses, not employees or workers of DoneStead. Each Partner Contractor is responsible for their own health and safety compliance, maintaining their own trade registrations, insurance and qualifications, and the professional standard of their own work. DoneStead's obligations under clause 7 (quality and defects) govern the Client's remedy — not any direct claim against a Partner Contractor.

2.4 Regulated activities — gas and electrical

All gas safety inspections and CP12 certificates are carried out by and issued by Gas Safe registered engineers. All Electrical Installation Condition Reports (EICR) and notifiable electrical work are carried out by NAPIT or NICEIC registered electricians. DoneStead coordinates access, scheduling and documentation. Certificates are issued directly by the registered engineer, who bears regulatory responsibility for their content, and are legally valid under the Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998 and the Electrical Safety Standards in the Private Rented Sector (England) Regulations 2020.

DoneStead does not and cannot accept liability for the regulatory accuracy of CP12 or EICR certificates — that liability rests with the issuing registered engineer.

2.5 Photo Assessments and Awaab Check — material limitations

The Photo Quote and Awaab Check services provide indicative assessments based on photographs submitted by the Client. The Client accepts the following material limitations by submitting photographs:

  1. Not a physical inspection. A photo-based assessment cannot replicate a physical survey. Conditions not visible in submitted photographs will not be detected.
  2. Not an HHSRS report. A DoneStead assessment does not constitute an HHSRS assessment, a PCA-standard damp survey, or any other form of professional regulatory report.
  3. Not a compliance certificate. An Awaab Check classification of "Low risk" does not confirm legal compliance with Awaab's Law, the Renters' Rights Act 2025, or any other regulatory requirement. The Client remains responsible for its own regulatory compliance.
  4. Not a substitute for expert advice. Where a property presents visible mould or damp, DoneStead recommends a physical survey regardless of the photo assessment outcome.
  5. Human review. All assessments are reviewed and approved by a member of DoneStead's coordination team before release. The Client has the right to request re-review of any assessment.

DoneStead's liability for Photo Assessments is limited as set out in clause 12.2.

2.6 B2B services

DoneStead's Services are intended exclusively for businesses, organisations and professional landlords acting in the course of a business. The Consumer Rights Act 2015 does not apply where the Client is acting in the course of a business. If a Client is in any doubt, they should seek independent legal advice before instructing DoneStead.

3. Quotations and Pricing

3.1 Published rates

Prices published on DoneStead's website are indicative near-fixed rates for standard jobs within our coverage area. They are not binding until confirmed in a written Quotation.

3.2 Written Quotations

A written Quotation is valid for 30 days from the date of issue. Acceptance of a Quotation by the Client constitutes an Instruction.

3.3 Price revisions

DoneStead may revise a Quotation where: (a) the scope differs materially from the description given; (b) unforeseen site conditions are discovered on attendance; or (c) material costs change materially between quotation and commencement. DoneStead will notify the Client in writing before proceeding.

3.4 Materials

Unless otherwise stated, published rates are for labour and coordination only. Materials are quoted, approved and invoiced separately, itemised at trade price plus a handling charge of 10%.

3.5 VAT

All prices are exclusive of VAT unless stated otherwise. Where VAT applies, it will be charged at the prevailing rate.

3.6 Congestion Charge

Central London Congestion Charge costs are included in DoneStead's standard rates and will not appear separately on any invoice.

4. Instructions and Contract Formation

A binding contract is formed when DoneStead confirms acceptance of a Client's Instruction in writing. Before commencing significant Works, DoneStead will provide a written scope confirmation. DoneStead will not proceed with material additional work without written Client approval. DoneStead reserves the right to decline any Instruction at its discretion.

5. Client Obligations

The Client shall: (a) provide accurate and complete property and scope information; (b) ensure safe, clear access for DoneStead and Partner Contractors; (c) notify DoneStead of known hazards, pre-existing defects or occupancy circumstances; (d) obtain all necessary consents and permissions before instructing Works; (e) ensure tenancy notice requirements are met; and (f) respond to DoneStead's communications within a reasonable time.

6. Payment Terms

6.1 Due date

Invoices are due for payment within 14 days of issue, unless a different period has been agreed in writing.

6.2 Late payment

DoneStead reserves the right to charge interest on overdue amounts at 8% per annum above the Bank of England base rate, and to recover reasonable debt recovery costs, under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998.

6.3 Disputed invoices

Where a Client disputes an invoice, the Client must notify DoneStead in writing within 7 days of receipt, setting out the nature of the dispute. Both parties will use reasonable endeavours to resolve the dispute within 14 days. The undisputed portion remains due on the original payment date.

6.4 Suspension for non-payment

DoneStead may suspend Services where a Client's account is overdue by more than 21 days, on 5 business days' written notice.

7. Works Completion and Quality

7.1 Photo evidence

DoneStead provides before-and-after photographic evidence for all Works, reviewed by DoneStead's coordination team before sharing with the Client.

7.2 Completion report

On completion, DoneStead provides a written completion report confirming the scope completed, contractors engaged, and any follow-up items.

7.3 Defect liability period

DoneStead warrants that Works will be free from defects in workmanship for 12 months from the date of completion. This warranty does not cover defects arising from fair wear and tear, misuse, occupant damage, or modifications by third parties.

7.4 Remedy for defects

Where a valid defect is notified within the defect liability period, DoneStead will arrange remediation at no additional charge, subject to reasonable access. Liability for defects is subject to the cap in clause 12.1.

All photographs, reports and other Deliverables produced by DoneStead remain DoneStead's intellectual property until the relevant invoice has been paid in full.

8.2 Client licence

On full payment, DoneStead grants the Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use Deliverables for the internal management of the relevant property.

11. Cancellation

11.1 Standard cancellation

Instructions may be cancelled without charge if written notice is given at least 48 hours before the scheduled attendance.

11.2 Late cancellation

Cancellations with less than 48 hours' notice may be subject to a charge of up to 30% of the agreed price, representing DoneStead's reasonable costs of scheduling and preparation.

11.3 Emergency call-outs

Emergency call-out instructions where a Partner Contractor is already en route or on-site may not be cancelled without full payment of the call-out fee plus costs incurred.

11.4 Cancellation by DoneStead

DoneStead may cancel without liability if circumstances outside its reasonable control prevent performance, if the Client has provided materially inaccurate information, or if safety concerns arise.

12. Limitation of Liability

✦ Revised v3.0: The liability cap has been restructured to address UCTA 1977 s.11 reasonableness requirements, introduce proportionate caps for physical works versus photo assessment services, specify excluded losses more precisely, and add proportionate liability and mitigation provisions.
This clause has been drafted to satisfy the reasonableness test under UCTA 1977 s.11 and Schedule 2. The cap structure is designed to be proportionate to both the value of individual instructions and DoneStead's public liability insurance cover. It should nonetheless be confirmed by a solicitor before publication, particularly in light of the principal model (clause 2.1) and the risk profile of the Awaab Check service.

12.1 Liability cap — physical works

DoneStead's total aggregate liability to the Client for any single claim or series of related claims arising out of or in connection with physical Works carried out under an Instruction shall not exceed the greater of:

  1. three times the value of the relevant Instruction; or
  2. £10,000.

This cap reflects DoneStead's role as coordinator and the fact that Partner Contractors carry their own professional indemnity and public liability insurance. Where a claim arises from the negligence of a Partner Contractor, DoneStead will use reasonable endeavours to assist the Client in bringing a claim directly against that Partner Contractor and their insurers.

12.2 Liability cap — Photo Assessments and Awaab Check

DoneStead's total liability for any claim arising out of or in connection with a Photo Assessment or Awaab Check assessment shall not exceed the greater of:

  1. the total fees paid by the Client to DoneStead in the three months preceding the event giving rise to the claim; or
  2. £5,000.

Why this cap is set at £5,000 minimum: Photo-based assessments are explicitly indicative only (see clause 2.5). The Client accepts that it is obtaining a preliminary screening tool, not a professional survey. Where a property is classified as Medium or High risk, DoneStead strongly recommends a physical damp survey before regulatory decisions are made. The Client's reliance on a photo-based assessment as a substitute for expert physical inspection is at the Client's own risk.

12.3 Excluded losses

DoneStead shall not be liable for the following, whether or not foreseeable at the time of the relevant Instruction:

  1. indirect or consequential loss;
  2. loss of profit, revenue, business or anticipated savings;
  3. loss arising from the Client's own failure to comply with regulatory obligations (including Awaab's Law or the Electrical Safety Standards Regulations 2020) other than where DoneStead has directly caused that failure;
  4. loss arising from the Client's reliance on a Photo Assessment without obtaining an independent physical survey where such a survey was recommended or clearly necessary;
  5. loss of data or documentation where the Client failed to maintain its own records.

12.4 Insurance

DoneStead maintains public liability insurance of at least £2,000,000 per occurrence. The liability caps in clauses 12.1 and 12.2 are set below this limit. Evidence of insurance cover is available on request. Where DoneStead holds professional indemnity insurance, that cover is available in connection with Photo Assessment and Awaab Check services. Evidence of PI cover, where held, is available on request.

12.5 Statutory exclusions — what cannot be limited

Nothing in these Terms limits or excludes DoneStead's liability for:

  1. death or personal injury caused by DoneStead's negligence or the negligence of persons for whom DoneStead is legally responsible;
  2. fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation;
  3. breach of the terms implied by section 12 of the Sale of Goods Act 1979 or section 2 of the Supply of Goods and Services Act 1982 (title and quiet possession);
  4. any other liability that cannot be lawfully excluded or limited under the law of England and Wales.

12.6 Mitigation

The Client must take all reasonable steps to mitigate any loss or damage suffered. DoneStead's liability will be reduced to the extent that the Client's own failure to mitigate has increased the loss.

12.7 Proportionate liability

Where a loss is caused by the combined fault of DoneStead and one or more Partner Contractors or third parties, DoneStead's liability shall be limited to its proportionate share of responsibility for that loss.

13. Force Majeure

DoneStead is not in breach and is not liable for delay or failure to perform where such delay or failure results from events outside its reasonable control, including severe weather, pandemic, civil unrest, government action, or unavailability of Partner Contractors or materials. This provision covers DoneStead's published emergency response time targets, which are subject to contractor availability. DoneStead will notify the Client promptly and use reasonable endeavours to resume performance.

14. Complaints

14.1 Notification

Complaints regarding the quality of Works must be notified in writing within 14 days of: (a) job completion; or (b) the date on which the defect was or reasonably should have been discovered — whichever is later.

14.2 Process

DoneStead will acknowledge complaints within 2 working days and provide a written response within 14 working days, including findings and any proposed remedy.

14.3 Escalation

Where a complaint cannot be resolved within 28 days, the parties may refer the matter to mediation before commencing legal proceedings.

15. Termination

15.1 Termination for cause

Either party may terminate an ongoing service arrangement immediately if the other is in material breach unremedied for 14 days, or becomes insolvent, enters administration, or ceases to trade.

15.2 Termination for convenience

Either party may terminate by giving 30 days' written notice. Confirmed Instructions before notice will be completed unless cancelled under clause 11.

15.3 Consequences

On termination: all outstanding invoices become immediately due; DoneStead delivers all Deliverables to which the Client is entitled; confidentiality obligations continue for 3 years.
