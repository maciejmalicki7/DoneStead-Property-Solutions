What DoneStead EOT Includes

Our EOT service is a fully managed coordination — not a direct clean. We handle the full scope so letting agents have zero overhead.

🧹 Professional Clean

Full end-of-tenancy clean to checkout standard by vetted partner company. Not sole traders — insured, accredited, with re-clean guarantee.

📷 Photo Condition Report

Before and after photography of every room. Every report is pre-screened for quality, then reviewed and approved by the DoneStead coordination team before the client sees it. Time-stamped and digitally archived.

🔧 Defect Coordination

Minor repairs, lightbulb replacement, lock checks, minor touch-ups. Identified during photo walkthrough and actioned without separate instruction.

🏛 Awaab Pre-Check

If any damp or mould signs are visible during EOT, a compliance pre-check is completed. Flagged to agent before new tenancy begins.

📋 Written Scope Confirmation

Written scope confirmed before works start. No surprises, no scope creep. Every instruction is documented and traceable.

✅ Same-Day Completion Report

Photo report and completion documentation sent same day as works finish. Your landlord client receives confirmation the same evening.
