Who We Are

DoneStead Property Solutions Ltd is a London-based B2B property maintenance coordination company. We work exclusively with property professionals — letting agents, portfolio landlords, housing associations, councils and build-to-rent operators — across Central and South East London.

We are not a cleaning company. We are not a direct contractor. We are the managed coordination layer between property professionals and a panel of vetted, accredited trade partners — providing documented evidence, compliance trails, and a consistent standard on every job.

Our Model

DoneStead operates a managed-contractor model. Every job is scoped, coordinated and photo-documented by our coordination team. Every photo report is reviewed and approved before it reaches you. Every certificate is issued by a Gas Safe or NAPIT/NICEIC registered engineer. Every payment is processed under HMRC's Construction Industry Scheme.

The result is a single point of contact, a single invoice, and a complete audit trail — without the overhead of managing multiple trade relationships yourself.

📋 Coordination, Not Contracting

DoneStead manages the instruction, access, trades and documentation. Partner engineers hold their own Gas Safe and NAPIT registrations — we coordinate, they certify.

📷 Photo-First Quality Control

Every job produces a before-and-after photo report. Our platform pre-screens for quality and completeness. The DoneStead team reviews and approves every report before you see it. Never automated, never unreviewed.

💷 Near-Fixed, Transparent Pricing

Our published rates are our starting point and are rarely exceeded for standard residential jobs. Congestion Charge built in. Materials invoiced separately and itemised. No hidden mark-ups.

⚖️ Awaab's Law Ready

Awaab's Law compliance is built into every damp and mould job. Documented remediation reports, time-stamped audit trails and compliance-ready PDFs prepared for regulatory inspection.

Technology & Quality

DoneStead combines operational experience with a technology-first approach to property maintenance coordination. Our platform provides photo evidence, job tracking and compliance documentation — giving property professionals real-time visibility without day-to-day involvement.

The DoneStead Pro portal gives portfolio managers and build-to-rent operators live contractor tracking, Available Now emergency dispatch, and portfolio-level health scoring. Our free Awaab Check tool allows letting agents and housing associations to upload damp photos and receive a compliance-ready assessment within 24 hours.

We believe that quality maintenance coordination should leave a paper trail. Every job we manage is documented, every photo is reviewed, and every report is audit-ready. That standard does not change whether the job is a single-appliance gas check or a full void refurbishment.
