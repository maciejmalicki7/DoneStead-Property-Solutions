Photo Submission Terms
& AI Assessment Notice

How DoneStead uses photographs you submit via Photo Quote and Awaab Check — and your rights regarding automated assessment.

Effective date: [INSERT DATE] Applies to: Photo Quote · Awaab Check · all photo submissions Last reviewed: July 2026

These terms apply whenever you submit photographs to DoneStead Property Solutions Ltd, whether via the Photo Quote service, the Awaab Check tool, the contact form, or any other channel. By submitting photographs, you confirm that you have read and agree to these terms.

Part A — Photo Submission Terms

1. What you confirm when you submit photos

By submitting photographs to DoneStead, you confirm that:

  1. You are the owner, managing agent, or authorised representative of the property shown in the photographs.
  2. You have the legal right to submit these photographs and to grant DoneStead permission to use them as set out below.
  3. Where photographs show the interior of an occupied property, you have obtained the informed consent of any tenants or occupants whose personal data — including their image, personal belongings, or identifiable health information — may be visible in the images.
  4. The photographs accurately represent the condition of the property at the time they were taken and have not been edited or manipulated to misrepresent the property's condition.
  5. No photographs contain images of children without the express consent of a parent or guardian.

2. How DoneStead uses your photographs

📷 Photo Quote

Used to prepare a price indication for the works shown. Reviewed by DoneStead's coordination team and, where necessary, shared with Partner Contractors for the purpose of scoping works.

💧 Awaab Check

Used to provide a damp and mould risk assessment under Awaab's Law. Pre-screened by our automated system, then reviewed and approved by a member of DoneStead's coordination team before the assessment is released to you.

📋 Job documentation

Photographs taken by DoneStead's Partner Contractors before and after Works are retained as a record of the property's condition and the work completed. Accessible via the Client Portal.

⚙️ Quality assurance

DoneStead may use photographs internally for quality assurance, team training and service improvement. Your photographs will not be published or used in advertising without your prior written consent.

3. What DoneStead will not do with your photographs

  • Sell photographs to third parties
  • Share photographs with any party not involved in delivering your instruction
  • Use photographs in advertising or marketing materials without your prior written consent
  • Use photographs for any purpose unrelated to the Services

4. How long we keep photographs

Photographs submitted by clients and taken during Works are retained for 7 years from the date of the relevant job, in accordance with our Privacy Policy and data retention policy. Where a legal dispute arises, photographs may be retained for longer.

5. Your rights over submitted photographs

You retain ownership of photographs you submit. You grant DoneStead a limited licence to use them only for the purposes described above. You may request deletion of photographs submitted in connection with an enquiry that did not proceed to a confirmed instruction, subject to any legal retention obligations. Contact info@donestead.co.uk to make a request.

Part B — AI Assessment Notice (UK GDPR Article 22)

This notice is provided in accordance with UK GDPR Article 22, which requires organisations to inform individuals when automated processing is used that produces significant effects, and to explain their rights in relation to such processing.

6. What automated processing we use

DoneStead's Awaab Check tool uses an automated pre-screening system to analyse photographs of damp and mould issues before they are reviewed by our coordination team. The automated system:

  • analyses image characteristics — visible mould growth, staining patterns, moisture indicators
  • produces a preliminary risk indicator (Low / Medium / High)
  • flags images that require closer human review

7. What the automated system does not do

The automated system does not make final decisions. It does not produce the assessment that is communicated to you. Every assessment is reviewed and approved by a member of DoneStead's coordination team before it is released. A human reviewer is always involved.

8. Significant limitations of photo-based assessment

You must be aware of the following before relying on an Awaab Check assessment:

  1. Not a physical inspection. A photo-based assessment cannot replicate a physical survey. Conditions not visible in submitted photographs will not be detected. A camera angle, lighting condition, or concealed area may result in an incomplete picture.
  2. Not an HHSRS report. A DoneStead assessment does not constitute a Housing Health and Safety Rating System (HHSRS) assessment or a PCA-standard damp survey.
  3. Not a compliance certificate. A classification of "Low risk" does not confirm legal compliance with Awaab's Law, the Renters' Rights Act 2025, or any other regulatory requirement. You remain responsible for your own regulatory compliance.
  4. Not a substitute for expert advice. Where a property shows any visible mould or damp, DoneStead recommends commissioning a physical damp survey regardless of the photo assessment outcome.
⚠ Housing Associations and Councils: Awaab's Law imposes strict response timelines. A photo assessment classified as "Low risk" should not be used as the sole basis for deciding not to inspect or remediate a property. DoneStead's assessment is a screening tool to help prioritise — it is not a substitute for a physical survey where damp or mould is visually present.

9. Your rights regarding automated assessment

Under UK GDPR Article 22, you have the right to:

  • Human review: Request that your assessment is reviewed by a human member of the DoneStead team. This is our standard process — every assessment is already reviewed by a human before release.
  • Explanation: Ask for an explanation of the factors that informed the assessment outcome.
  • Re-assessment: Request a re-assessment if you believe the initial assessment is inaccurate or based on insufficient photographic information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact info@donestead.co.uk with your assessment reference. We will respond within 5 working days.

10. Complaints about assessments

If you believe an Awaab Check assessment is inaccurate, contact info@donestead.co.uk within 14 days of receiving it, setting out your concern and providing any additional photographs or information. DoneStead will review the assessment and respond within 5 working days.

11. Liability for assessments

DoneStead's liability for photo-based assessments is limited as set out in our Terms & Conditions (clause 12.2). Reliance on a photo assessment as a substitute for a physical inspection, where such an inspection was recommended or clearly necessary, is at the Client's own risk.
